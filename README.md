\# Slidronix — AI-Based Landslide Early Warning System



Slidronix is an AI-based landslide risk assessment and early-warning research project focused on the Western Ghats of India.



The project combines historical landslide inventories with terrain, satellite, soil, and rainfall information to construct a multimodal dataset and develop machine-learning and deep-learning models for landslide risk prediction.



> \*\*Current status:\*\* Phases A–E completed. Phase F (Deep Learning) is the next development stage.



\---



\## 1. Project Objective



The objective of Slidronix is to develop a data-driven framework that can:



\- identify environmental characteristics associated with landslides,

\- combine multiple geospatial and environmental data sources,

\- predict landslide occurrence/risk,

\- compare traditional machine-learning models with deep-learning approaches,

\- generate spatial risk information for future GIS-based visualization and early-warning applications.



The current implementation is a research prototype. The final real-time early-warning system is planned for later phases.
